Get the CFPM Conference App

This app is shared directly with conference attendees — you won't find it by searching the App Store or Google Play. Just follow the steps below for your phone.

📱 iPhone
Step 1 of 1 — Install the app
Download for iPhone

This opens the App Store. Tap the blue "Get" or "Install" button. That's it — the app icon will show up on your home screen in a minute or two.

🤖 Android

The Android app is shared privately too, which means it takes two taps instead of one. Do them in order, one after the other. You'll only ever need to do this once.

Step 1 of 2 — Join the group
Join the group

This opens a page from Google Groups. Tap the blue "Join group" button on it. If it asks you to sign in first, use your Gmail / Google account.

Already done this before? You can skip straight to Step 2.

Step 2 of 2 — Install the app
Download the app

This opens a page from Google Play. Tap the blue link that says "download it on Google Play" — it's circled below so you know exactly what to look for. On the next screen, tap the green "Install" button, exactly like installing any other app.

Does it say you're not a member yet? Go back and finish Step 1 first, then come back to this page and tap Step 2 again.

Once it's installed, future updates happen automatically — you won't need to repeat these steps.
