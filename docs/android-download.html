Get the CFPM Conference App

The Android app is distributed as a private test build, not a public Play Store listing. It takes two quick taps to get it — do them in order the first time.

Step 1 of 2
Join the testing group

Already done this before? Skip straight to Step 2.

Step 2 of 2
Download the app

If this says you're not a member yet, go back and do Step 1, then come back to this page and tap Step 2 again.

Why two steps?

Google Play only lets private test builds through to people who belong to an approved group. Step 1 puts you in that group; Step 2 is the actual install, which only works once Step 1 is done. You only need to do this once — after that, Play Store updates to the app happen automatically like any other app.
