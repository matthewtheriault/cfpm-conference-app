CFPM Conference App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This app is published by the Canadian Federation of Podiatric Medicine (CFPM) for attendees of its annual conference. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Your name. When you first open the app, you're asked for your first and last name to personalize the experience (e.g. "Welcome, Jordan!"). This stays on your device only — it is never sent to our servers.
  • A push notification token. If you allow notifications, your device registers an Expo push token so we can deliver conference announcements. This token is stored with your device's platform (iOS/Android) and isn't linked to your name or any other personal information.
  • Anonymous usage data tied to a random device ID. The app generates a random identifier on your device (not your phone's hardware ID or advertising ID) to remember things like which polls you've already answered and which sessions you've checked into. This ID isn't linked to your name.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account, email, or password to use the app.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

The app relies on a small number of infrastructure providers to function:

  • Google Firebase — stores conference content (schedule, speakers, exhibitors, etc.) and the anonymous data described above.
  • Cloudinary — hosts photos/logos uploaded by conference organizers (speaker headshots, sponsor logos, maps). This doesn't involve attendee data.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications using the token described above.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

Admin access

Conference organizers manage app content (schedule, speakers, notifications, etc.) through a separate admin sign-in, restricted to a single authorized account. This is unrelated to attendee use of the app.

Your choices

You can disable notifications at any time in your device's system settings. Uninstalling the app removes the name and device ID stored on your device. To request removal of your push token or poll/check-in data from our servers, contact us using the details below.
